Icon options E and F: macOS craft

The designer's angle: form, material, light and depth that belong beside the best Mac apps. The two rows at the bottom of the image place the icon among other app icons to judge whether it stands out.
E · Run key (designer's pick)
Idea: A keycap seated in a recess, carrying the letter R: pressing Command-R to run. The recess is the yard.
Colour: Light aluminium tile, dark recess, vermilion keycap, white R.
Weak points: May suggest a keyboard utility; the joins in the R can still be refined; the R is only just legible at the smallest size.
F · Lit yard
Idea: A ring of translucent glass walls seen from above, with a glowing cursor standing on the sunken dark floor and warm light on the floor and inner walls.
Colour: Indigo tile, amber light.
Weak points: The bar may read as a letter I, a 1 or a power switch; indigo sits close to the blues and purples of many developer tools.
