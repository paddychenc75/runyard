Design foundations

Two layers: content sits below and is opaque; navigation and controls float above it in glass. Glass is for floating layers only, never for the content itself.

Glass material

Light glass · Toolbar, tabs
Dark glass · Same spot in the dark theme
Blur 28 to 50, saturation 180%, a thin highlight stroke and a soft drop shadow. Larger floating layers use a stronger blur.

Motion
Floating layers scale up from the button that triggered them, in about 0.25 s, with a slight bounce. Startup lights up in four steps: copy, install, start, ready. With “Reduce motion” on, they cross-fade instead.
Legibility
Text on glass is always solid, never translucent. With “Reduce transparency” on, glass becomes an opaque light or dark gray and the layout does not change.
