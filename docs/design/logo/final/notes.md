# Runyard icon, final set: "Current Line"

Polish of round-3 candidate D. Same concept: lines of code on a warm tile, top lines executed (solid ink), one thick orange line executing and leaving the tile to the right, the line below pending.

All SVGs are generated by `gen.py` (masters) and `final_set.py` (chosen parameters, pixel-fitted small sizes, glyphs). `mk.py` writes the PNG exports, `dock.png` and `sheet.png`. Everything was rendered with `rsvg-convert` and judged from the renders. Exploration renders are kept in `work/` (`e1`–`e5`, `c1`–`c4`, `verify.png`, `v1024.png`).

## Decisions for the eight points

1. **Overshoot / motion.** The gradient direction is reversed compared with round 3: ember red at the tail (left), brightest yellow-orange at the leading tip, so the head is the hottest point. A soft cast shadow sits under the bar only where it is over the tile and gets stronger toward the tile edge; outside the tile there is no shadow, so the bar reads as passing above the edge. Overshoot lengths 32, 60 and 88 units past the edge were rendered side by side (`work/e3.png`): the short one looks like a knob stuck on the edge, the long one looks like a deliberate exit. Chosen: 80 units (tip at x=1004), the longest that still leaves a 20-unit margin in the 1024 canvas. A fading (transparent) tail was also tried (`work/e4.png`, `e5.png`): it reads more as a streak but turns pink on the white tile, hides the indent of the line and looks airbrushed, so it was rejected in favour of the solid bar.
2. **Executed / pending pair.** Tested lighter solid versus heavier outline in five layouts (`work/e1.png`). The outline still goes faint at 64 and 32 px; the lighter solid stays readable at every size and looks intentional. Pending is now a solid bar in a light tint of the same ink. Four rows (2 executed, 1 executing, 1 pending) chosen over five thicker rows: at 64 and 32 px four rows separate clearly, five start to crowd, and the short stray bottom bar is gone. Bars are thicker: 76 units (was 62), executing 108 (was 92).
3. **Tile.** Warm white, nearly flat gradient, no bevel and no white inner highlight. Edge is a 4-unit inner hairline (8-unit stroke clipped to the tile) plus a soft drop shadow. Checked on #F5F5F7 and #FFFFFF: the boundary is visible on both at 1024 down to 16 px.
4. **Indent rhythm.** Indents are 0, 1, 2, 0 with lengths 264, 430, (executing), 372: in, in, then fully back out, like a block closing. Compared with 0-1-1-0, 0-1-2-1 and others (`work/e1.png`, `e2.png`); 0-1-2-1 still read as a chevron, 0-1-2-0 with a long last bar does not.
5. **Small sizes.** `icon-32.svg`: four rows on whole pixels (3, 3, 5, 3 px high), executing bar 5 px and running 2.5 px past the tile. A three-row 32 px was also drawn and compared at true size (`work/c3.png`); four rows stayed legible and keep the same structure as the master, so four was kept. `icon-16.svg`: three rows (2, 4, 2 px, 1 px gaps), tile 14 px, bar runs 1 px past the tile. Both have a 1 px rim and a hard 1 px cast shadow, no blur.
6. **Dark appearance.** Warm charcoal tile, same geometry. Executed bars are a toned-down cream (not white) so the orange line is the brightest element; pending is a dark grey; the executing bar has a brighter head and a soft glow (overshoot version only). The tile separates from #1C1C1E by a light hairline rim.
7. **Dock-safe.** `icon-dock.svg` / `icon-dock-dark.svg`: bar drawn past the edge and clipped by the tile, so it bleeds off the right side with a square cut and the brightest colour at the edge. Rim is drawn under the bar so the bleed is clean. The mock Dock (`dock.png`) clips the artwork with the same squircle used as the system mask, on a light and a dark wallpaper, at 200 px and 64 px tiles, next to plain coloured squircles. It also shows the light master in a dark Dock and the overshoot master clipped by the mask (it survives, but loses its rounded tip and keeps a shadow under a bar that no longer overhangs; use the Dock-safe file instead).
8. **Monochrome glyph.** 18-unit grid: solid 3 px bar (executed), 5 px bar that runs off the right edge with a square cut (executing), hollow 4 px bar with a 1 px stroke (pending). States differ by fill, weight and exit, not by tone. Checked in black and white at 18 px, 36 px and enlarged.

## Similarity screen

`similarity2/report.md` did not exist when the design work was done; it appeared before finalising and was read then. Its advice and what was done:

- "Reduce to four rows", "make the orange bar differ by shape, not only colour (thicker)", "break the tidy symmetric staircase": these were already in the design (four rows, executing bar 1.4x thicker and leaving the tile, non-symmetric 0-1-2-0 indent).
- Changed because of the report: bar lengths made more irregular (first row 290 to 264, second 420 to 430, pending 350 to 372; in the 16 px icon ink 6 to 5 px and pending 5 to 7 px; in the 32 px icon 9/11/10 to 8/12/11) so the top and bottom rows do not mirror each other.
- Kept as advised: overshoot, warm white plus orange, only one lit line.
- Avoided as advised: no bullets or handles at the left of bars, no extra colours, no play triangle, no text, no carbon/metal texture on the dark tile (it is flat).
- Not done: a caret or cursor mark at the left end of the orange bar (the brief excludes prompt-like marks).
- Open risk from the report that this set does not remove: the dark variant is "dark tile with an orange bar", the same family as OmniOutliner's icon, although flat and with only one orange line.

## Colours

Light: tile #FFFEFC to #F5F2EB (top to bottom); rim #6E6350 at 30 %; tile shadow #2A2010 at 20 %; ink #24232B; pending #C4C1C6 (#B4B1B8 in the 16/32 px icons); executing gradient #E8451A (0) → #FA6A1A (0.6) → #FF9A22 (0.86) → #FFC94A (1); cast shadow #7A2A00, 5 % to 42 %.

Dark: tile #34312F to #1D1C1B (#2A2827 flat in the 16/32 px icons); rim #FFFFFF at 16 %; executed #D6D1C8; pending #5F5C58 (#75716B small); executing gradient #EA4A1A → #FF7A1A (0.55) → #FFA62A (0.85) → #FFDA6E; glow #FF7A1A at 55 %, blur 30.

Geometry (1024 grid): tile 100–924, corner radius 185.4 with the smoothed-corner bezier construction; bars start at x = 232 + indent x 88; rows at y 275, 397, 519 (executing), 673.

## Still weak

- The motion cue is modest. Bright head plus cast shadow make the bar read as "lit and passing over the edge", but it is still a static pill; some people will still see a slider. A stronger cue (fading tail) was rejected for cleanliness.
- In the Dock-safe version the "leaving" idea is carried only by the bar touching the edge with a square cut. It is clearly the same icon, but less distinctive than the overshoot.
- Without colour the glyph is close to a generic indent / align-left symbol (also the main finding of the similarity screen). The heavy bar running off the edge is the only real differentiator.
- The hollow pending bar in the glyph has a 1 px interior at 18 px @1x and is soft there; fine at @2x.
- The pending bar is a cool grey on a warm tile. It is in the ink's colour family as asked, but it is the least warm element.
- The 16 px icon uses a 14 px tile (larger than the 824/1024 proportion) to fit three rows; next to icons that keep the standard margin it will look slightly bigger.
- The tile path is repeated inside each SVG (clip, shadow, fill, rim) rather than referenced with `<use>`; editing the tile shape means regenerating with `gen.py`.

## Not verified

- No test on a real Dock, Finder, Launchpad or menu bar, and none on real macOS icon masking; `dock.png` is a drawn simulation with the mask assumed to equal the 824 tile squircle. How current macOS treats an icon with content outside the mask (clip versus shrink onto a plate) was not checked.
- Whether and how macOS will pick the dark variant automatically depends on how the app ships its icon (Icon Composer / asset catalog); not tested.
- The squircle is the widely used smoothed-corner bezier approximation, not Apple's official template; not compared against the official grid.
- Rendering was checked only with `rsvg-convert` (blur filters and gradients may differ slightly in browsers or Quick Look). No `.icns` was built.
- Sizes viewed: 1024, 512, 128, 64, 32, 16 on #F5F5F7, #FFFFFF and #1C1C1E, as flat backgrounds only; no real wallpapers apart from the two mock gradients.
- The similarity report's own limits apply (no SF Symbols, several Apple app icons not obtained).
