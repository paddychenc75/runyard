Icon options C and D: brand concept

The designer's angle: the mark carries one idea. He reads "yard" as a rail yard: many tracks side by side, and a switch that sends one of them out to run. "Run" is the lit route, with no play triangle.
C · Switchyard (designer's pick)
Idea: One lead track splits into three; one of them is a glowing green route laid over the two grey ones.
Colour: Graphite tile, grey tracks, signal green.
Weak points: At first glance it resembles the "branch" icon common in code tools; dark with green leans towards a terminal look; lines get fussy at the smallest size.
D · Lowercase r that is also a switch
Idea: A short dark stem with a curve rising from its foot and turning right. The shape is both a lowercase r and a rail switch.
Colour: Warm off-white tile, the curve running from amber to vermilion.
Weak points: At first it is only a letter r and the switch needs explaining; the dark stem nearly vanishes at the smallest size.
