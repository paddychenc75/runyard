Icon A developed further: three variants with a second reading

All keep the single-stroke R and the baseline that runs off the right edge. Left to right: the earlier version, variant one, two and three. Each column shows the bare black sign above and the icon at several sizes below.
Earlier version
The form chosen in the last round, shown for comparison.
Weak point: No second reading.
Variant one · Two lanes
Stroke width, the white inside the R and the gap between stem and leg are all equal, so the white space becomes a path too: the black line runs out, the white lane runs in.
Weak point: The most reserved idea, hard to notice unprompted; closest to the common rounded monoline R.
Variant two · Passing through (designer's pick)
The line enters from the left edge, writes an R and leaves by the right edge, with no start and no end. The tile is the yard and the R is the route through it. Two icons side by side join up.
Weak point: The R takes a moment longer to read; without the tile the shape can read as J and R joined.
Variant three · Route
The stem runs out through the bottom edge and the baseline out through the right: the line enters the yard from below and leaves to the right.
Weak point: The fastest R to read, but the second reading is weaker.
