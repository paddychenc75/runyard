Refining the current line: four versions, D chosen

Left to right: the original, A, B, C, D. Each column, top to bottom: on light and on dark (each with 128, 64, 32 and 16 px), then single colour. The last strip places the small icons next to media player, text-align, notes and terminal icons.
A · Edge and words
The tile gets a defined edge, lines are split into word-like segments, the lit line is heavier.
Weak point: At small sizes the segments turn into scattered dots and add noise.
B · Charcoal, glowing
A charcoal tile where the lit line really glows and the line above keeps some warmth. The strongest sense of being lit.
Weak point: It leaves the warm flat style; a dark tile sits closer to terminals and editors.
C · Gutter
A line-number gutter on the left and a pale orange band across the executing line, as a debugger draws it.
Weak point: It reads as a selected row in a list.
D · The line really leaves (chosen)
The lit line extends past the tile and breaks the outline, which still shows at 16 px; pending lines are hollow, so the single-colour version separates the three states without colour.
Weak point: Recent macOS versions fit every icon into the system rounded square, so the overshoot may be clipped; the Dock needs a version where the line stops at the edge. Not yet verified on a real system.
