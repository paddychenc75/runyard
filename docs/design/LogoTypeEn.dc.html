Icon options A and B: reductive mark

The designer's angle: the fewest strokes that still read as this product. In the image, the top row is the bare mark in black, below are light and dark backgrounds, and the run at the lower right is each size from large to small.
A · Run-on R (designer's pick)
Idea: One even stroke drawn in a single pass that never crosses itself. The bowl is left open, like a yard with a gate; the baseline runs off the right edge of the tile, for "run". It still reads as an R.
Colour: Lime tile, near-black stroke.
Weak points: A few people may first read other letters; the open bowl is lost at the smallest size; lime has no link to the blue used in the interface.
B · Block R
Idea: A solid block with one large rounded corner, cut three times to make an R.
Colour: Ink tile, warm white letter.
Weak points: Familiar, close to "a letter in a square"; the small notch at the waist is unclear; a dark tile loses its edge on dark backgrounds.
